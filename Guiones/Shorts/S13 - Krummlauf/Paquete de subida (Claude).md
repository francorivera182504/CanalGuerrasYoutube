## TITLE
How This Rifle Fired Around Corners

## DESCRIPTION
In 1945, Germany fielded the Krummlauf: a curved barrel attachment for the StG 44 that let a soldier fire from cover while aiming through a mirror sight. The 30° version was for infantry and the 90° version for tank crews. The cost: the curve wore out after roughly 300 rounds. Animated reconstruction. Sources: Small Arms Review, Royal Armouries. #ww2 #history #weapons
