**TITLE:** This Pilot Fought 30 Enemy Planes Alone
Alternativas: One P-51 vs 30 German Fighters · The Lone Mustang Who Saved the Bombers

**DESCRIPTION:**
On January 11, 1944, Major James H. Howard lost contact with his group over Germany — then saw more than 30 German fighters attacking American bombers. Alone in his P-51 Mustang, he attacked for about 30 minutes, even after three of his four guns stopped firing. He received the Medal of Honor.
Animated reconstruction. Source: official Medal of Honor citation (U.S. Air Force).
