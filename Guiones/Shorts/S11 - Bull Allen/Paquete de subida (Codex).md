## DESCRIPTION
On 30 July 1943 at Mount Tambu, New Guinea, Australian stretcher-bearer Leslie “Bull” Allen rescued 12 wounded Americans under fire and earned the Silver Star.
Sources: AWM https://collection.awm.gov.au/C34760 | Anzac Portal https://anzacportal.dva.gov.au/resources/wau-salamaua-1942
