**TITLE:** This Medic Saved 75 Men Without a Gun

**DESCRIPTION:**
In May 1945 on Okinawa, U.S. Army medic Desmond Doss refused to carry a rifle. When a Japanese counterattack drove the Americans off the Maeda Escarpment, he stayed — lowering wounded men down the cliff one by one. He saved about 75 and received the Medal of Honor.
Animated reconstruction. Sources: U.S. Army, National WWII Museum, Library of Congress.
