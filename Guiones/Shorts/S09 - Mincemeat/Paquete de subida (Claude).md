# S09 — Paquete de subida (Claude)

**TITLE:** The Dead Man Who Fooled Hitler
Alternativas: Operation Mincemeat in 45 Seconds · The Fake Officer Who Never Existed

**DESCRIPTION:**
In 1943, British intelligence gave a dead man a fake identity — Major William Martin — with love letters, theatre tickets and a briefcase of fake secret letters. A submarine released him off Huelva, Spain. The papers reached the Germans and pointed to Greece and Sardinia. Hitler reinforced the wrong places, and in July the Allies landed in Sicily.
Animated reconstruction. Sources: Royal Navy Museums, National WWII Museum, IWM.
