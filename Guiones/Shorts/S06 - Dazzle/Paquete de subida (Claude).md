Why WW1 Ships Were Painted Like Zebras

## DESCRIPTION
You're a U-boat captain in 1917, and your target is painted like a giant zebra. Dazzle camouflage didn't hide ships — it was meant to confuse your guess of their speed and heading.

Animated reconstruction. Britain painted roughly 3,000 ships in dazzle by late 1918, and the US painted about 1,250.
