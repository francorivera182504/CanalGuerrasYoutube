## DESCRIPTION
```text
Why is a war machine named after a water container?

In late 1915, Britain needed an innocent cover name for a secret armoured vehicle. "Landship" said too much, so the project became the "tank". Some were even marked in Russian: "With care to Petrograd." Its first battle came at Flers on 15 September 1916.

Animated reconstruction. Sources: The Tank Museum; National Army Museum; Albert Stern, Tanks 1914–1918 (1919).
```
