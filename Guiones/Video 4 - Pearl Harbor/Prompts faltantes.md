Si abres un chat nuevo, pega primero este bloque de estilo:

```
For every image in this chat: 16:9 horizontal, whiteboard Draw My Life style, pure white background, simple black marker stick figures with round heads and dot eyes, clean marker lines, lots of white space, flat accent colors only (American side = blue, Japanese side = red, flames orange, smoke grey). Recurring character Stick the reporter: round head, dot eyes, smile, olive-green military helmet with a small PRESS label, dark blue vest with PRESS on the chest, small notebook. No extra text unless asked. No blood, no gore.
```
