## Neutralidad: nueve ajustes concretos

- **101a — “For Jews…” / “the whole problem”:** homogeneiza a los judíos, reduce el relato palestino al arraigo rural y simplifica las causas. Escena completa EN: “For many Jews, this was an ancestral homeland and a hoped-for refuge from persecution. Palestinian Arabs also had deep ties to the land, where their families had lived for generations, and sought national self-determination. Both peoples claimed a national future in the same place.”
- **201a — “Israel saw a war coming”:** convierte una percepción gubernamental en certeza colectiva. EN: “Israeli leaders feared a wider war.”
- **205a — “Each side lived the conflict as the victim”:** presenta daños reales como una identidad psicológica uniforme y simétrica. EN: “Civilians in both societies faced fear, loss and insecurity.”
- **304 — “Iran's fight and the Palestinian fight…”:** confunde la política iraní con todas las aspiraciones palestinas. EN: “Iran's new leaders made support for the Palestinian cause part of their foreign policy.”
- **402 — “Palestinian bombings…” / “Israeli military operations…”:** explicita la violencia palestina pero diluye sus consecuencias del lado israelí. Sustituir ese tramo por EN: “Palestinian bombings and shootings killed Israelis, including civilians; Israeli military operations killed Palestinians, including civilians.”
- **605a — “strikes on Hezbollah”:** acepta sin atribución la identificación israelí de los blancos. EN: “Israeli strikes in Lebanon continued, with Israel saying it was targeting Hezbollah.”
- **703a — “Iran blamed Israel and promised revenge”:** deja a Irán como acusador y oculta la autoría posteriormente reconocida. EN: “Iran blamed Israel and promised retaliation; Israel's defence minister later acknowledged the killing.”
- **904 — solo voz gubernamental iraní:** a diferencia de israelíes y palestinos en 205a/406a, los iraníes carecen de una perspectiva civil cotidiana. Escena completa EN: “Iran's government says its enrichment is peaceful and demands an end to sanctions, blockade and attacks. Iranian families also face threats to their safety and livelihoods.”
- **905 — “each side's security is the other side's threat”:** atribuye una lógica única a todos y omite derechos y decisiones políticas. EN: “Because disputes over security, sovereignty, territory and regional power remain unresolved.”
