## 1. 1947–1949: partición, War of Independence y Nakba
- UN General Assembly Resolution 181 recommended Jewish and Arab states with an internationally administered Jerusalem — 1947-11-29 — vote: 33 for, 13 against, 10 abstentions — [ONU, cronología](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-207490/).
- The Jewish Agency accepted partition; the Arab Higher Committee and Arab states rejected it. Palestinian Arab opposition invoked majority self-determination; Jewish leaders accepted a route to statehood — 1947 — 2 proposed states; a recommendation, not an implemented border settlement — [ONU, informe de 1949](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/703168/files/A_648-EN.pdf), [ONU, historia](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-208097/).
- Israel declared independence; neighboring Arab armies entered the former mandate after months of civil fighting — declaration 1948-05-14; interstate war from 1948-05-15 — founding of 1 state; the proposed Palestinian Arab state did not emerge — [Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1945-1952/arab-israeli-war).
- Approximately 700,000–750,000 Palestinians fled or were expelled; Palestinians call the dispossession the Nakba, meaning “catastrophe.” The Israeli commemorative name for the war is “War of Independence” — 1947–1949 — approximate range, not an exact census — [CRS, aproximadamente 700,000](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/RL/PDF/RL34074/RL34074.47.pdf), [BBC, aproximadamente 750,000 y ambos nombres](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/ckgr71z0jp4o).
- After the war, Israel controlled about 77% of former mandate Palestine west of the Jordan; Egypt administered Gaza and Jordan controlled the West Bank, including East Jerusalem — 1949 — 3 controlling states; no sovereign Palestinian state — [ONU, historia](https://www.un.org/unispal/history/).

## 2. 1967–1993: territorios, asentamientos, Intifada y Oslo
- Israel captured the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, from Jordan; Gaza and Sinai from Egypt; and the Golan Heights from Syria — 1967-06-05–10 — 6-day war — [ONU, cronología](https://www.un.org/unispal/timeline/).
- Israel withdrew from Sinai under its peace treaty with Egypt — treaty 1979; principal withdrawal completed 1982-04-25 — Sinai must not remain colored as currently Israeli-controlled on a historical-to-present map — [ONU, anuario de 1982](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/auto-insert-201387/).
- The First Intifada began as a Palestinian uprising against Israeli occupation — 1987; continuing into the Oslo period — N/A; distinguish it from the Second Intifada in 2000 — [ONU, historia](https://www.un.org/unispal/history/).
- Israel and the PLO exchanged recognition letters: the PLO recognized Israel; Israel recognized the PLO as representative of the Palestinian people — 1993-09-09; Oslo declaration signed 1993-09-13 — 2 distinct recognitions; Israel did not thereby recognize a Palestinian state — [CRS contemporáneo](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/93-797.html), [Office of the Historian](https://2001-2009.state.gov/r/pa/ho/time/pcw/97181.htm).
- Oslo established a framework for interim Palestinian self-government, implemented through the Palestinian Authority from 1994; permanent issues remained for negotiation — 1993–1995 — intended interim period: 5 years; not a final peace treaty — [CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/93-797.html), [estudio institucional](https://ciaotest.cc.columbia.edu/conf/cfr19/cfr19f.html).
- Recent published counts put settlers in the West Bank **including East Jerusalem** at approximately 737,000–779,000, using different 2024 reference dates/data — UN report 2025; PCBS publication 2026 using end-2024 data — 737,332 versus 778,567; **not** a November 2026 census or statistical confidence interval — [ONU, A/80/356, §22](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?l=en&s=A%2F80%2F356&t=pdf), [PCBS](https://www.pcbs.gov.ps/portals/_pcbs/PressRelease/Press_En_LandDay2026E.pdf).
- **DISPUTADO, posición jurídica:** the ICJ's advisory opinion found Israeli settlements contrary to international law; Israel rejected the opinion and maintained that the conflict requires a negotiated settlement — 2024-07-19 — non-binding advisory opinion, separate from the Gaza genocide case — [CIJ/ONU](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/icj-pressrelease-19jul24/), [Reuters, declaraciones de ambas partes](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/top-un-court-says-israel%27s-occupation-of-palestinian-territories-is-illegal/84283878).

## 3. 1979: del entendimiento con el sha a la confrontación
- Under the shah, Iran and Israel maintained strategic, intelligence and military cooperation, despite Iran not granting full de jure recognition — before 1979 — 2 cooperating states, not a formal public alliance treaty — [Encyclopaedia Iranica](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/israel-i-relations-with-iran/).
- The Islamic Revolution removed the shah's government and made opposition to Israel part of the new leadership's policy — 1979 — transition from monarchy to Islamic Republic — [Reuters, cronología](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/iran-and-israels-open-warfare-after-decades-of-shadow-war-3386043).
- Iran severed relations with Israel and handed its former Israeli embassy to the PLO — 1979-02 — 1 embassy changed diplomatic occupants — [estudio académico, British Journal of Middle Eastern Studies](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13530194.2019.1661646).
- During Israel's invasion of Lebanon, Iranian Revolutionary Guards helped establish Hezbollah and subsequently supplied training and weapons — 1982 onward — founding year: 1982; the organization developed over time — [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/iran-and-israels-open-warfare-after-decades-of-shadow-war-3386043), [USIP](https://iranprimer.usip.org/resource/iran-and-israel).
- The political rupture did not instantly end every clandestine connection: Israel remained a source of Western arms during the early Iran–Iraq war — early 1980s; war 1980–1988 — useful qualification to “they became enemies overnight” — [USIP/PBS](https://cgi.pbs.org/wgbh/pages/frontline/tehranbureau/2010/10/iran-primer-iran-and-the-palestinians.html).

## 4. 2000–2022: Gaza y el conflicto nuclear

- Hamas won the Palestinian legislative election, then seized Gaza after fighting Fatah; the PA continued governing parts of the West Bank — election 2006-01-25; takeover 2007-06 — Hamas: 74 of 132 seats; parliamentary victory, not a separate Gaza presidential election — [Parlamento británico](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm200607/cmselect/cmintdev/114/11406.htm), [PBS/Frontline](https://www.pbs.org/wgbh/frontline/article/timeline-israeli-palestinian-peace-process-1993-oslo-accord/).
- Israel tightened Gaza's blockade after Hamas's takeover; Egypt restricted its own Rafah border. **DISPUTADO, justificación:** Israel cited security and weapons concerns; Palestinians described collective punishment — from 2007 — 2 bordering states, with different controls; avoid portraying one identical joint blockade mechanism — [CRS, bloqueo y posiciones enfrentadas](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R41275.html).
- Major Israel–Gaza wars preceded 2023: December 2008–January 2009, July–August 2014 and May 2021; another round occurred in November 2012 — 2008–2021 — approximately 22 days, 50 days and 11 days respectively for the three requested wars — [Reuters, cronología](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/conflict-between-israel-and-palestinians-in-gaza-3192941), [CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R47754.html).
- Stuxnet targeted uranium-enrichment machinery at Natanz, bringing cyber operations into the confrontation over Iran's nuclear facilities — publicly identified in 2010 — 1 computer worm; avoid presenting its developers as officially established by this source — [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/iran-and-israels-open-warfare-after-decades-of-shadow-war-3386043).
- The JCPOA exchanged nuclear limits and verification for sanctions relief; Trump withdrew the U.S. and restored sanctions — agreement 2015-07-14; U.S. exit 2018-05-08 — enrichment cap under the deal: 3.67% — [OFAC, acuerdo](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/iran-sanctions/joint-plan-of-action-jpoa-archive-and-joint-comprehensive-plan-of-action-jcpoa-archive), [AP, límites](https://apnews.com/article/5d3aeae869e77069ef8146251c0a8aa9), [CRS, salida](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/20180509_R44942_32fa286bdd186559c215d8df080d812246b6480b.html).
- **DISPUTADO, autoría:** nuclear scientist Mohsen Fakhrizadeh was assassinated near Tehran; Iranian leaders blamed Israel, which did not claim responsibility at the time — 2020-11-27 — 1 scientist; do not date this killing to 2021 — [BBC, noticia contemporánea](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4-x4sLgZyGs), [Axios, atribución y falta de reconocimiento](https://www.axios.com/2020/11/28/irans-supreme-leader-revenge-assassination-mohsen-fakhrizadeh).

## Términos neutrales
- Usar «1948 war / Israel's War of Independence» y explicar «Nakba» como desposesión y desplazamiento palestinos; evitar tratar ambos conceptos como idénticos en toda su extensión.
- Usar «Israeli government / Iranian government / Hamas / Palestinian Authority» según el actor; evitar convertirlos en «los judíos», «los iraníes» o «los palestinos» colectivos.
- Usar «occupied West Bank, including East Jerusalem», con contexto jurídico; distinguir control de facto, anexión declarada y soberanía reconocida en los mapas.
- Si aparece el lugar sagrado: «Haram al-Sharif / Temple Mount»; Al-Aqsa es una mezquita del recinto, no un sinónimo geográfico exacto de todo el monte.
- Usar «killed», «reported deaths», «hostages», «prisoners/detainees» con categorías precisas; evitar «eliminated», «martyrs», «human animals» o llamar rehenes a todos los presos.
- Usar «Iran-backed» cuando haya apoyo documentado; «axis of resistance» como autodenominación atribuida; evitar asumir órdenes iraníes en cada ataque.
- Usar «South Africa alleges genocide / Israel denies it / ICJ provisional measures»; evitar «ICJ convicted Israel». CIJ y CPI son tribunales distintos.
- Distinguir «Famine, IPC Phase 5» territorial de «Catastrophe, IPC Phase 5» de hogares; distinguir bloqueo de puertos iraníes de restricciones al tránsito por Hormuz.
